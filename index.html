Welcome

Thank you for visiting my website. I am a Business major concentrating in Management Information Systems (MIS), the field where business strategy and technology meet. I built this site to share my background, my professional goals, and a little about who I am outside of the classroom and the workplace.

Whether you are a recruiter, a professor, a classmate, or simply curious, I hope this site gives you a clear picture of what I bring to a team and where I am headed.

Why Management Information Systems?

Every organization runs on information. Orders, schedules, inventory, and customer feedback all have to reach the right people at the right time, and the systems that move that information can make or break a business. MIS appeals to me because it focuses on solving practical problems: understanding how a business operates, finding where things slow down, and using technology to make work faster, more accurate, and easier for the people doing it.

I am especially interested in databases, business analytics, and the design of systems that real employees actually enjoy using.

What I Bring

My work history began in the dish pit and has grown into a role as an expeditor at JOEY Bellevue, a high-volume restaurant where timing and communication matter every minute of a shift. Along the way I have learned to stay calm under pressure, communicate clearly with coworkers, pay close attention to detail, and take ownership of the final product. These are the same qualities I plan to bring to a career in business and technology.
